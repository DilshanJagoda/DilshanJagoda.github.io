# e_class - Teacher Edition

## Overview

e_class Teacher Edition is a full-stack student management application designed for individual teachers to manage students from multiple grades, classes, and learning locations.

The system provides a digital solution for handling student information, payment records, and reporting workflows while reducing dependency on manual paperwork.

The application was designed with flexibility in mind, allowing teachers to manage their own student groups without requiring a complete institutional setup.

---

## Problem Statement

Individual teachers often manage students from different grades and locations while maintaining records manually.

This creates challenges such as:

- Maintaining accurate student information
- Tracking attendance history
- Monitoring payment status
- Generating organized reports

e_class was developed to simplify these daily administrative tasks through a centralized digital platform.
